Typewriter ASCII

Art you type by hand. Every space is a keystroke.

What to type

Silhouettes and line drawings survive the trip to a typewriter. Group photos do not.

The picture is spelled out with this sentence, repeating over and over. It reads straight through, line after line, so someone can actually read your picture.

Higher separates the drawing from the background. A typewriter has no greys to fall back on, so a flat picture turns to mush.

Lower smooths the picture down before it is turned into characters. Texture cannot survive a cell this size, and leaving it in reads as dirt.

One line of letters per line you type. A blank line leaves a gap.

Spacing is kept exactly as given. Characters your machine cannot type are swapped for the nearest shape it has, and you will be told which.

Your machine

Fewer characters, calmer picture.

Measure your machine

Type 40 capital M. Measure the left edge of the first to the left edge of the last?

Optional: type 40 lines of capital M. Measure the top of the first to the top of the last.?

The paper

Wider means more detail and a great many more keystrokes.
