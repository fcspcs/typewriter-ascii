Typewriter ASCII

Art you type by hand. Every space is a keystroke.

What to type

Silhouettes and line drawings survive the trip to a typewriter. Group photos do not.

The picture is spelled out with this sentence, repeating over and over. It reads straight through, line after line, so someone can actually read your picture.

One line of letters per line you type. A blank line leaves a gap.

Spacing is kept exactly as given. Characters your machine cannot type are swapped for the nearest shape it has, and you will be told which.

    Keeping your hands on the machine
    
    
      Space, Enter or ↓ moves to the next line, ↑
      goes back. That is all the app needs, which is why a
      Bluetooth camera shutter remote works — the ten-euro
      kind sold for phone selfies. They enumerate as a keyboard and send a
      space or a volume key, so pair one, tape it where your wrist rests or
      put a foot switch under the desk, and press it at the end of each line.
      Nothing to install and nothing to configure.
    
    
    
    
      That is worth doing even if you also use listen. The
      microphone counts strikes within a line; the button is what guarantees
      the line itself is right, and a wrong line is the error that ruins a
      sheet.
